# Shuaa results status

The first Kaggle campaign completed on 2026-09-18. Its selected two-seed ConvNeXt V2 Tiny ensemble achieved 85.0716% validation accuracy and 73.7819% Macro-F1 in training. The [measured report](runs/training-20260918-024537/measured-report.md) and [provenance](runs/training-20260918-024537/provenance.json) preserve the evidence. The submitted ONNX is evaluated independently for the live leaderboard; test remains untouched. The [next-experiment research](improvement-plan.md) motivated the second campaign below.

The improvement campaign completed on 2026-09-22. Its selected equal-probability mean of two ConvNeXt V2 Tiny models, pretrained with ImageNet-22K then ImageNet-1K weights and fine-tuned with mixup alpha 0.1, measured **86.2712% validation accuracy** and **75.7370% Macro-F1** in training on 3,001 images. This is +1.20 and +1.96 percentage points relative to the first ensemble, respectively. The [second measured report](runs/training-20260922-205413/measured-report.md) lists all seven trials, the [provenance](runs/training-20260922-205413/provenance.json) fixes the dataset and chosen runs, and the [ONNX parity gate](runs/training-20260922-205413/model.parity.json) passed. Both chosen runs completed 31 of 35 configured epochs before their 1.25-hour per-run limit. These are validation measurements; test remains untouched.

The gain is not uniform: validation recall for Soiling increased by 12.9 percentage points and Vegetation by 8.9, while Hot-Spot recall fell by 16.2 points on only 37 Hot-Spot validation images. The frozen DINOv2 Small head reached 74.2752% accuracy, below the ConvNeXt trials. Compare [the live independently evaluated record](../public/data/leaderboard.json) for the published score, and read the limitations in [methodology](methodology.md) before presenting results as a research claim.

No trained accuracy is hard-coded in this repository. Read the live benchmark records for independently evaluated scores.

The training runner generates the measured report, epoch logs, validation predictions, ONNX parity check and provenance in `shuaa-output/` after an actual Kaggle run. Until those artifacts exist, the corresponding experiment is pending. A CPU synthetic-fixture smoke test verifies the implementation only and is not a solar-dataset result.

Report each real run with its architecture, seed, weighted/unweighted loss, epochs, stopping reason, validation accuracy and Macro-F1. Keep final test scores separate and record the locked finalist IDs and split hash. Include limitations from [the methodology](methodology.md) when presenting results to the instructor.
